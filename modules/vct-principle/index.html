What Air-Pot Has Already Proven

Air-Pot is not only a container. It is a proven air-pruning architecture.

Air-Pot has earned its position by turning a simple principle into a working product family: shaped wall geometry, air access, drainage and repeatable root-tip interruption. The system is practical, manufacturable and already understood by growers.

Their practical engineering focus Correct filling, wall shape, drainage and aeration are presented as practical conditions for reliable air pruning. This is a clear grower-facing explanation.

The new cone design helps the growing medium fall into the angled cone shape, making the known process easier to use.
